How the radar prices a launch

For each category, take the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in that category (three or more of them; otherwise the whole snapshot’s median of ). Then:

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × maturity factor

  • Growth factor = 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +100%) ÷ 100 × 0.4
  • Maturity factor = 0.70 + min(age in days, 1460) ÷ 1460 × 0.30 (age unknown: 365 days)
  • Watchlist = the snapshot’s own 40-protocol floor (TVL ≥ $50M, no token recorded, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product). The board adds every other rankable pre-token protocol below that floor — the early radar the watchlist alone would miss.
